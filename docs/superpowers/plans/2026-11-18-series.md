# A series – several books with one Workshop

The proposal `przeglad/seria/seria-propozycja.md` (18.11) with its two mockups: a series is one
project with several books (volumes) and one Workshop. The format of the project file has had a
list of books since format 2 (decision of 9.10); the program has used its first book only.

What each book has of its own: its text (sections and elements), its data (title, author,
language, sections, captions, notes, bibliography style, ISBN, publisher), its cover and its texts
for publishers, its target length. What the books share: the Workshop (cards, the book dictionary,
mind maps, timelines, work notes, clippings, sources), the paragraph styles, the record of writing.

The work comes in five pull requests.

## Stage 1 – the core (no change on the screen)

1. **The name of the series.** `BookProject::series`: `"series"` in the `.klh` file, left out when
   empty. `ProjectManager::seriesName()` (empty: the title of the first book) and
   `setSeriesName()`.
2. **The current book.** `ProjectManager::book()` is the current book: the first one until
   `setCurrentBook(bookId)` chooses another (`currentBookChanged()`); a project opened again
   starts with its first book. `currentBookIndex()`, `isSeries()` (more than one book) and
   `bookOf(elementId)` (the book that has the element; none for the Workshop);
   `BookProject::bookIndex(bookId)` and `bookIndexOf(elementId)`. Everything that takes a part
   of "the book" takes the part of the current book: adding elements, the places of Add
   Element, the reading order of the statistics.
3. **A place in another book.** A place (`ElementPlace`) stays as it is: the book it is in is a
   separate `bookId` parameter, empty for the element's own book, so a project with one book
   has the places it had.
4. **A new book.** `ProjectManager::addBook(NewBook)`: its title, author and language, its place
   among the books, and its start: the first chapter only, or also the elements of the front and
   back sections of the book before it (the title page anew, with the new title; the others with
   their text). Its folder is the first free one of `book2`, `book3`... One save of the project.
5. **Order and removal.** `moveBook(bookId, index)`; `removeBook(bookId)`, never the last
   book: the links to its elements and sections go with it, as `removeElement()` takes them,
   and the elements of the Workshop linked to it keep their other links; the files of its
   elements stay on the disk for the window to put in the Trash, as removing a chapter does.
6. **Moves between books.** `moveElementTo(elementId, place, bookId)` and
   `canMoveTo(elementId, place, bookId)` with `bookId` of another book: the element and the
   elements inside it go to that book; their files stay where they are. `movePlacesOf()` gives
   the places of the element's book, as now; `movePlacesIn(elementId, bookId)` the places in
   another book.
7. Tests: `tests/core/test_series.cpp`.

## Stage 2 – the Navigator and the windows

The volumes in the tree under the name of the series ("Tom 1: Piasek"), the current one in bold;
the window New Volume (Book > New Volume...); the menu of a volume (Earlier, Later, Volume
Properties, Delete Volume...); the Properties panel of the series (its name, the table of the
volumes) and of a volume; the title of the window. The windows that show the places of the book
(Add Element, Move, Add to Book, Import, the drops of the Navigator) show those of the current
volume, or of the volume of the element. Tests: `tests/gui/test_series_window.cpp`.

## Stage 2b – moves between the volumes

An element dragged to another volume, or moved there with Move... and its list of the volumes
(`movePlacesIn()`, `moveElementTo(..., bookId)` of stage 1); the volumes dragged in the
Navigator.

## Stage 3 – the tools of a volume

Export and print with the list Volume; statistics of the volume and of the series; the plot board
and the outline of a volume; Find in Book in the volume or in the series; the cover and the texts
for publishers of each volume, and texts of the whole series.

## Stage 4 – joining and splitting

A Kalahari book joined to a series as a volume, with its Workshop merged; a volume taken out of a
series as a book of its own.
