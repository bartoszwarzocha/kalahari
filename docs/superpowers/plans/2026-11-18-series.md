# A series – several books with one Workshop

The proposal `przeglad/seria/seria-propozycja.md` (18.11) with its two mockups: a series is one
project with several books (volumes) and one Workshop. The format of the project file has had a
list of books since format 2 (decision of 9.10); the program has used its first book only.

What each book has of its own: its text (sections and elements), its data (title, author,
language, sections, captions, notes, bibliography style, ISBN, publisher), its cover and its texts
for publishers, its target length. What the books share: the Workshop (cards, the book dictionary,
mind maps, timelines, work notes, clippings, sources), the paragraph styles, the record of writing.

The work comes in five pull requests.

## Stage 1 – the core (no change on the screen)

1. **The name of the series.** `BookProject::series`: `"series"` in the `.klh` file, left out when
   empty. `ProjectManager::seriesName()` (empty: the title of the first book) and
   `setSeriesName()`.
2. **The current book.** `ProjectManager::book()` is the current book: the first one until
   `setCurrentBook(bookId)` chooses another (`currentBookChanged()`); a project opened again
   starts with its first book. `currentBookIndex()`, `isSeries()` (more than one book) and
   `bookOf(elementId)` (the book that has the element; none for the Workshop);
   `BookProject::bookIndex(bookId)` and `bookIndexOf(elementId)`. Everything that takes a part
   of "the book" takes the part of the current book: adding elements, the places of Add
   Element, the reading order of the statistics.
3. **A place in another book.** A place (`ElementPlace`) stays as it is: the book it is in is a
   separate `bookId` parameter, empty for the element's own book, so a project with one book
   has the places it had.
4. **A new book.** `ProjectManager::addBook(NewBook)`: its title, author and language, its place
   among the books, and its start: the first chapter only, or also the elements of the front and
   back sections of the book before it (the title page anew, with the new title; the others with
   their text). Its folder is the first free one of `book2`, `book3`... One save of the project.
5. **Order and removal.** `moveBook(bookId, index)`; `removeBook(bookId)`, never the last
   book: the links to its elements and sections go with it, as `removeElement()` takes them,
   and the elements of the Workshop linked to it keep their other links; the files of its
   elements stay on the disk for the window to put in the Trash, as removing a chapter does.
6. **Moves between books.** `moveElementTo(elementId, place, bookId)` and
   `canMoveTo(elementId, place, bookId)` with `bookId` of another book: the element and the
   elements inside it go to that book; their files stay where they are. `movePlacesOf()` gives
   the places of the element's book, as now; `movePlacesIn(elementId, bookId)` the places in
   another book.
7. Tests: `tests/core/test_series.cpp`.

## Stage 2 – the Navigator and the windows

The volumes in the tree under the name of the series ("Tom 1: Piasek"), the current one in bold;
the window New Volume (Book > New Volume...); the menu of a volume (Earlier, Later, Volume
Properties, Delete Volume...); the Properties panel of the series (its name, the table of the
volumes) and of a volume; the title of the window. The windows that show the places of the book
(Add Element, Move, Add to Book, Import, the drops of the Navigator) show those of the current
volume, or of the volume of the element. Tests: `tests/gui/test_series_window.cpp`.

## Stage 2b – moves between the volumes

An element dragged to another volume, or moved there with Move... and its list of the volumes
(`movePlacesIn()`, `moveElementTo(..., bookId)` of stage 1); the volumes dragged in the
Navigator (`NavigatorPanel::volumeDropIndexOf()`). Tests: `tests/gui/test_series_window.cpp`.

## Stage 3 – the tools of a volume

1. **Notes and captions.** Each volume has its own settings of the footnotes and of the numbers
   of the captions (a new volume copies those of the volume before it); a text is numbered among
   the texts of its own volume, whichever is current. Tests: the `[series]` cases of
   `tests/gui/test_caption_coordinator.cpp` and `tests/gui/test_footnote_coordinator.cpp`.
2. **Export and print.** In a series the windows of the export and of the print have the list
   Volume on their tab Texts: the texts, the file name, the data of an EPUB (title, author,
   language, ISBN, publisher) and its set for a publisher are those of the volume chosen.
   Tests: `tests/gui/test_export_book_dialog.cpp`, `tests/gui/test_print_book_dialog.cpp`.
3. **Statistics and goals.** Each volume has its own length, deadline and deadlines of its parts;
   the first volume keeps the goals of the book. The book view of the statistics counts the
   current volume and has the row Words in the whole series; the record of writing is one.
   Tests: `tests/core/test_writing_goals.cpp`, `tests/gui/test_writing_goals_tab.cpp`.
4. **Plot board and outline.** They show the texts of the current volume, with the list Volume
   above them; Show on Plot Board of a text of another volume switches to it. Tests:
   `tests/gui/test_series_window.cpp`.
5. **Find in Book.** The current volume, or every volume with the button Series (Replace All in
   Series); the results name the volume of each text. Tests: `tests/gui/test_book_search.cpp`.
6. **The cover and the texts for publishers.** A material of the cover and a text for
   publishers belong to a volume (`ProjectElement::bookId`, `"book"` in the `.klh` file) or to
   the whole series (`core::VolumeMaterials`). A book that becomes a series gives them to its
   first volume; a new one belongs to the current volume; a volume removed leaves its materials
   to the whole series, the chosen ones as drafts. One chosen takes the place of the others of
   its place in its own volume only; a volume without one uses the whole series' one. Each
   volume has its own set for a publisher. The Properties panel has the field Volume, the lists
   the column Volume and groups of each volume; the plan of the cover, the view of the set and
   the export follow the volume. Tests: `tests/core/test_volume_materials.cpp`,
   `tests/gui/test_series_cover.cpp`.
7. **Titles.** The fields of a text and its version history name the volume of the text; the
   Dashboard, the window Card Fields and the book dictionary name the series, whose volumes
   share the Workshop. The style of the bibliography is the volume's own (stage 1). Tests: the
   `[series]` cases of the tests of the fields, the versions, the Dashboard, the card fields,
   the book dictionary and `tests/core/test_series.cpp`.

## Stage 4 – joining and splitting

A Kalahari book joined to a series as a volume, with its Workshop merged; a volume taken out of a
series as a book of its own. Both copy: the book or the series they start from stays as it was.

1. **A volume taken out** (the menu of a volume: Take Out as a Book...).
   `ProjectManager::takeOutBook(bookId, parentDir, createSubfolder)` writes a new project and
   does not open it: the volume as its only book, its texts and other files in `book/` (a file
   of another volume's folder under a free name), its images and the versions of its files;
   the Workshop with the same ids, without the materials of the cover and the texts for
   publishers of the other volumes (those of the whole series and its own become the book's),
   without the links to the other volumes; the kinds, the type (and `types/`), the styles, the
   book dictionary; the goals of the volume in its project.db. The record of writing stays with
   the series. The window then deletes the volume from the series when the writer asks (Delete
   Volume, its files to the Trash) and opens the new book in a window of its own.
2. **A book joined as a volume** (Book > Join Book as Volume...). The book chosen is read, not
   opened. It has one book, the type of the series (or both are user projects: the kinds join),
   and no id of the series (a book taken out and still in the series). Its book is a new volume
   at the place chosen, in a free folder (`book2`...), with its files, images and versions. Its
   Workshop joins the series': an element with an id of the series is the same element (a book
   taken out before), the series keeps its own and gains its links; a card of the same kind and
   title is the same card when the writer ticks it in the window (each pair ticked at first),
   and the links and portraits that name it name the series' card; the other elements come at
   the end of the Workshop, their files in `workshop/` under free names, with their versions.
   Its materials of the cover and texts for publishers are for the new volume. The dictionary
   joins, the styles and the fields of the cards of the series stay (those it lacks come
   along), its goals are the volume's; its record of writing stays in its own project.db. The
   window (JoinBookDialog) says why a book cannot join, lists the pairs of cards and the places
   among the volumes (at first the last one); the changes of the book in another window of the
   program are saved before it joins, and when they cannot be, it does not join.
3. Tests: `tests/core/test_series_join.cpp`, `tests/gui/test_series_join.cpp`.
